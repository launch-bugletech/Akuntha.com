Technical planning

What we review before installation

These considerations shape the installation plan. Actual requirements are confirmed against equipment information and the approved design.

01

Equipment size and rating

Transformer weight, dimensions and voltage ratings influence handling equipment, space requirements and connections to the power system.

02

Foundations and access

The foundation, approach route, lifting space and installation clearances must be checked against the equipment drawings and site conditions.

03

Earthing and connections

Earthing, cable or busbar interfaces and protection arrangements must match the approved electrical design and assigned work scope.

04

Cooling and site conditions

Ventilation, drainage, oil containment and fire protection provisions depend on the transformer type and project requirements.

05

Manufacturer instructions

Handling restrictions, accessory assembly and installation tolerances should follow the manufacturer’s approved requirements.

06

Checks and handover

Installation inspections, documentation and commissioning interfaces are planned according to the tests and responsibilities agreed for the project.

FAQs

Questions about transformer installation

Need to discuss a specific transformer or site?

Ask our team ↗
01What information is needed for an installation quotation?

Transformer rating, voltage class and type, approximate weight, site location, foundation drawings, access route details, and the scope you expect from us.

02Do you handle transformer unloading and positioning?

Equipment movement, unloading and lifting-plan coordination are part of our work packages. Crane and access needs are confirmed at site review.

03Can mounting be provided without full Substation EPC?

A standalone installation package may be possible. We confirm responsibilities and interfaces with the appointed contractors before work begins.

04How are foundation and access requirements assessed?

At site review we check plinth dimensions and level, the approach route, lifting space, oil containment and fire clearance against the transformer drawings.

05Does the service include earthing and cable connections?

Earthing, cables, and panel or bus connections are included where they are agreed in the scope of work.

06Who is responsible for testing and energization?

That is set by the contract and by the licensed parties involved. We support pre-energization checks and documentation within approved scope.

07Can you support replacement at an operating facility?

Often, yes. The plan depends on access, outage windows and existing interfaces, which we assess before committing to a schedule.

08Where does Akuntha provide transformer installation services?

We are based in Gandhinagar, Gujarat and work with clients across India. Share your location and we will confirm availability.
